Investor Overview · Core Platform Acceleration Case

Energy-orchestrated digital infrastructure that turns contracted power into compute, flexibility and AI-ready capacity.

BitMach converts contracted industrial power access into programmable compute capacity, grid flexibility and AI-ready data-centre infrastructure. The business is not defined by any single compute use case — it is defined by access to scarce, grid-connected power and the ability to present that load to the grid as controllable and dispatchable.

The case presented here is the Core Platform Acceleration Case (Layers 1–4). Owned-GPU inference (Layer 5) is held as a separate, optional venture — ring-fenced in its own SPV if and when it is pursued.

Core Infrastructure Thesis

The scarce asset is controllable power — not the chip

South Africa's grid no longer suffers primarily from a shortage of generation; it suffers from a shortage of controllable, real-time demand. The AI market needs power-secure sites. BitMach sits between the two as an energy-orchestration layer, monetising the same power platform across multiple revenue layers.
